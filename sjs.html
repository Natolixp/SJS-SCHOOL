SAINT JOSEPH SCHOOL ADDIS ABABA

St. Joseph School (SJS) in Addis Ababa is renowned for its exceptional academic rigor, consistently graduating students who achieve the country's best academic performance. The school boasts an outstanding record of success, with a near-perfect rate of graduates gaining admission to national and prestigious international universities, including Ivy League institutions like Princeton and Harvard. This academic prowess is complemented by the school's robust extracurricular programs in athletics and the arts, which cultivate well-rounded and socially engaged young men. This holistic approach to education and its legacy of producing successful alumni make it one of the most sought-after and respected educational institutions in Ethiopia.
